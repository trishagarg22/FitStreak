Exercise Guide

Important Safety Instructions:-

  • Warm up first: spend 5 to 10 minutes on light movement and gentle stretching before you start.
  • Cool down after: Finish with slow movement and stretching to let your heart rate settle.
  • Form before speed: Good technique matters more than the number of reps. Start with the Beginner plan if you are unsure.
  • Progress slowly: Increase reps, sets or difficulty gradually, not all at once.
  • Stay hydrated: Sip water before, during and after your workout.
  • Have a Balanced Diet: Maintain a Healthy Diet and consult your Nutricianist
  • Rest and recover: Take at least one or two rest days a week and get enough sleep.
  • Avoid exercising straight after a heavy meal. Wait a couple of hours.
  • Consult your Doctor in case of any mishappening.

*NOTE: It is a rough guide, not medical advice.
